NBA model v1 — independently tested

Frozen Elo + home court + B2B rest logistic model; no paid data needed. Brier measures probability accuracy (lower is better). Model was trained on 2016–2023, checked on 2024–25 and evaluated once on an untouched 2025–26 holdout.
The market performed better on the untouched season. A positive displayed model-vs-odds edge is not a validated betting advantage; all new NBA picks remain research/paper-tracking only.
